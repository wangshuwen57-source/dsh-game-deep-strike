# Deep Strike

**Helios Mining Station has been silent for 71 hours.** Command sent only you.

## How it plays

A first-person pixel shooter. Each sector holds three energy cores scattered across different rooms — clear the enemies, collect the cores, and only then does the extraction point light up.

- **12 sectors** — 6 hand-built layouts, the last 6 rearranged; three visual themes (cold grey / rust / bio-green)
- **6 weapons** — pistol, shotgun, SMG, rocket launcher, plasma, grenade launcher; each with its own fire rate, sound and recoil
- **10 enemy types** — lunging grunts, ranged shooters, kamikaze bugs, flyers, splitters, shield bearers, and a three-phase boss
- **In-run builds** — kills drop weapon modules; carry up to 3 (piercing, explosive, crit, quick-draw…)
- **Cover and barrels** — crates block bullets but break, red barrels explode and chain
- **Daily challenge** — 4 rule sets rotating by date (frenzy / pistol only / fragile / swarm)

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard & mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | drag left half | WASD |
| Look | drag right half, or slide while holding fire | mouse |
| Fire | hold the fire button | left click |
| Switch weapon | ◀ ▶ buttons | 1–6 / Q E |

## Story

12 dialogue scenes and an 11-part ending. The thing inside learns — first the voice, then the shape. Once it has learned, it doesn't need to learn anymore.

The **Story** entry on the main menu replays every line; six collectible archive entries are scattered through the sectors.

## Length

A run takes 15–25 minutes; full collection about 2 hours.
